Engineered for Flawless Audio

Everything you need to fix corrupted audio, quiet tracks, or uneven panning.

True Mono Downmix

Accurately computes equal 50/50 blend of Left and Right audio signals, sending identical sound to both headphone drivers.

Left / Right Channel Cloner

When one channel is filled with static or completely silent, copy the good channel to both ears and mute the broken one entirely.

300% Volume Booster

Can't hear a soft-spoken teacher or quiet YouTube interview? Boost gain up to 3x without breaking tab audio fidelity.

Stereo Channel Swap

Invert left and right output channels instantly. Perfect if headphones are reversed or video audio was rendered inverted.

YouTube SPA Compatible

Detects YouTube dynamic page navigation (`yt-navigate-finish`) so your settings stay active across playlist videos and recommendations.

Instant Keyboard Hotkey

Press Alt + Shift + M anytime to toggle mono mode on or off without reaching for your mouse.

Install in 30 Seconds

No Chrome Web Store account needed. Load the source code directly into your browser!

1

Download Repository

Download the ZIP or clone the repository to your computer:

git clone https://github.com/YOUR_USERNAME/mono-audio-fixer.git
2

Open Extensions Page

Navigate to your browser's extension management page:

  • Chrome & Brave: chrome://extensions
  • Edge: edge://extensions
  • Opera: opera://extensions
3

Load Unpacked

Enable Developer mode (top-right toggle), click Load unpacked (top-left), and choose the extension folder. You're ready to go!
